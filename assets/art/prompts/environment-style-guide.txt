Limits Breaker environment art direction

Render cinematic, high-detail dark-fantasy game environments with weathered charcoal stone, muted slate and steel, restrained burgundy, contained violet arcane light, and warm amber practical light. Match the established menu, dungeon, boss-arena, and forge plates: realistic material texture, layered depth, filmic contrast, soft atmospheric perspective, and controlled volumetric light. Keep the central horizontal band quiet and readable beneath the game UI.

Avoid flat cel shading, thick outlines, anime features, vector-like surfaces, bright white washes, neon, logos, lettering, symbols that resemble writing, and interface elements. These scene plates are intentionally opaque full-frame PNGs; transparency is for cutout characters, icons, and effects.
